Tap J or Attack for your character's melee move: Marco alternates punches and kicks, Mario kicks forward, and Donkey kicks backward. Start alone; hit recruitment boxes from below and touch their rewards. Recruited companions follow independently and attack nearby bugs automatically; tap K or Helpers to request an attack. Helpers is unavailable until a recruit is active. Release and tap again after each cooldown. Intact bricks block short-range strikes. Stranded companions regroup after a delay. Recruitment survives checkpoint recovery but resets on a new run. Use the blaster for the boss core.
